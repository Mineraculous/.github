# Mineraculous

Welcome to **Mineraculous**! 

Mineraculous brings the universe of *Miraculous: Tales of Ladybug & Cat Noir* into Minecraft, featuring Miraculouses, Kwamis, Kamikotizations, abilities, suits, custom tools, and extensive addon customization support.

## Repositories

- [**Templates**](https://github.com/Mineraculous/Templates) — The official files used for assets.
- [**Resources**](https://github.com/Mineraculous/Resources) — The official asset and data files shipped by the mod.
- [**Addon-MDK**](https://github.com/Mineraculous/Addon-MDK) — The official Mod Development Kit (template) for building custom Mineraculous addons.
- [**.github**](https://github.com/Mineraculous/.github) — Organization health, shared workflows, and dependency management tooling.
